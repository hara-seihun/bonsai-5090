## Integer grid removes FP64 without inventing source semantics

Every source half-scale has an exact2^-10 lattice coordinate:

- E4M3 code below8: coefficient=code.
- Otherwise exponent=code/8, mantissa=8+code%8; coefficient=mantissa×2^(exponent−1).
- Weighted doubled partial contribution=g2×coefficient. Its magnitude is at most44,040,192.

`native_mixed_whole_grid_fixed.cu` accumulates those coordinates as signed32 integers. After each group of four, it truncates the magnitude to24 significant bits and restores the sign. Four carrier-producing instructions still meet only in the joint observer; this is **not decoder-free**, because the final observer interprets g2/u2.
